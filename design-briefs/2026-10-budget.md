## 3. Unit economics

A user on the proposed R149 monthly plan (about $8) costs us roughly **R50–85 a month**
(R35–50 for the bridge plus R15–35 for voice and AI). So each user contributes about R65–100 a
month. Extra broker accounts at R49 a month cost us about R27–36 (MetaRPC's $1.50–2.00 per
extra account), so each is also profitable. One-off R599 buyers are the loss-makers, which is why
the move to monthly matters.
