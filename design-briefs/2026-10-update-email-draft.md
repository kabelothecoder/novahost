Subject: What's coming to NovaHost, and what stays the same

Hi there,

We've got a round of updates coming to NovaHost over the next few days, and we wanted you to hear about them from us first.

**What's changing**
- **A faster, steadier connection to your broker.** We're moving your robot's link to a new connection provider. You may be asked to re-link your trading account once. It takes about a minute.
- **A smoother iPhone setup.** New iPhone users will pick a plan, pay, then enter their licence key, all in one flow.
- **Chart scanner packs.** Everyone gets 1 free scan a month, and you can top up with a pack of 20 scans for R150.
- **Notifications and voice.** Trade alerts on iPhone, plus an optional voice assistant you can talk to.

**What stays the same**
- Your licence, your robot and your settings stay as they are.
- Nothing trades without your rules. Voice trade ideas always wait for you to tap to confirm.
- If you've already paid, you won't be charged again for anything you already have.

If anything looks off after an update, just reply to this email and we'll sort it out with you.

Thanks for trading with NovaHost.
